Idle Carousel

Keep the carousel spinning! Leaves, sticks and rocks blow in from every side and latch on. Put animals on the carousel to sweep them away, earn Gold, and restore the park.

Coming soon to Steam

For Windows. A free demo first, the full game in 2027.

Your carousel does the work

Early on you're clicking debris away. Then a Wolf sweeps, a Sloth makes everything sleepy, a Panda patches things up, and soon they're clearing the whole park without you.

Keep it on your desktop

Desktop mode shrinks the game into a little round window you can leave spinning in a corner while you work.

Bosses and seasons

Six bosses to stamp on your Season Ticket, then start a new season with Golden Tickets to spend at the Prize Booth.

About Corbett's Castle

Hi! I'm a solo dev, and my daughters love carousels. There aren't many carousel games out there, so I'm making them: Idle Carousel first, and more carousel games in other genres after.

Made in Godot. The art is drawn in code or from Kenney's free packs; the music is by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), and the sounds are from Kenney. No AI art, music or voice. AI coding assistants help with the code, and AI helped draft some English text and the translations.
