Idle Carousel

Keep the carousel spinning! Leaves, sticks and rocks blow in from every side and latch on. Put animals on the carousel to sweep them away, earn Gold, beat the bosses, and bring the old park back to life.

Coming soon to Steam

For Windows. A free demo first, the full game in 2027.

Your carousel does the work

Early on you're clicking debris away. Then a Wolf sweeps, a Giraffe reaches far out, a Sloth makes everything sleepy, an Elephant swats whatever gets close, and a Panda patches things up. Soon they're clearing the park without you.

Bosses that fight back

Six bosses stand between you and a finished season, from a swirling Leaf Storm to an Overflowing Bin. They hit the carousel back, so build it tough before you take them on.

Restore the park

Every season earns Park Supplies. Spend them to bring the old park back: string lights, flower beds, a fountain, a balloon seller and more stands, one by one.

Seasons and prizes

Stamp every boss on your Season Ticket, then start a new season with Golden Tickets to spend at the Prize Booth. Stub, a very excited ticket, shows you around.

Challenges and the Crowd

Want it harder? Pick challenges for a season, like tougher bosses or a tighter budget. Every one draws a bigger Crowd, and a bigger Crowd pays more Golden Tickets.

Keep it on your desktop

Desktop mode shrinks the game into a little round window you can leave spinning in a corner while you work.

About Corbett's Castle

Hi! We're a two-person studio, and our daughters love carousels. There aren't many carousel games out there, so we're making them: Idle Carousel first, and more carousel games in other genres after.

Made in Godot. The art is drawn in code or from Kenney's free packs; the music is by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), Écrivain and Matthew Pablo; and the sounds are from Kenney and Freesound. No AI art, music or voice. AI coding assistants help with the code, and AI helped draft some English text and the translations into eight languages.
